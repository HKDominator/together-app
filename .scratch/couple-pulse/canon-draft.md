## Bright together

1. Music loud in the kitchen, cook something ridiculous together.
2. Open the windows and dance badly until somebody laughs.
3. A walk with no route, take every turn you have never taken.
4. Dessert first tonight. Dinner can wait its turn.
5. Sit outside until the streetlights come on, like you have nowhere to be.
6. Plan the next free weekend out loud, the sillier the better.
7. Trade the best moment of your day over something cold on the doorstep.
8. The board game from the back of the shelf, winner picks breakfast.

## Quiet day for both

1. Tea on the balcony, phones inside.
2. Early pajamas, soft lamp, no plans.
3. Read in the same room, no need to talk.
4. A slow lap around the block before bed, coats over pajamas.
5. Warm soup, low light, leftovers count.
6. A whole album from start to finish, lying down, eyes closed optional.
7. Couch and blankets, whoever reaches the kettle first makes both cups.
8. Hot shower, clean sheets, lights out early. Tomorrow gets the rest.

## Carrying it together

1. Sit close for a while. Nothing needs saying tonight.
2. The simplest dinner you both like, eaten side by side.
3. A short walk arm in arm, no destination.
4. One pot of something warm, two spoons, the couch.
5. A candle at dinner, even if dinner is toast.
6. An early night together. The dishes can lose this one.
7. Shoulder to shoulder at the kitchen table, whatever is left of the evening.
8. Hot drinks made the way you each like them, and no fixing anything tonight.
