# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A couple — two partners sharing a private workspace. They use Together throughout the day to coordinate shared tasks, check in on progress, and communicate. Context: personal, often on a laptop at home or a phone between meetings. They are not power users of productivity software; they want something that feels made for them, not adapted from a work tool.

## Product Purpose

Together is a private shared workspace for two partners. It lets them create and assign tasks, track progress with statistics, chat in real time, and manage their shared life from one place. Success looks like both partners opening the app daily without friction, trusting it to hold their shared commitments, and feeling like it knows them — not like they're using a generic tool they happen to share a login for.

## Brand Personality

Playful, warm, modern, reliable. The voice is personal and direct, like a note left by someone who knows you. It does not speak in corporate abstractions. It acknowledges the relationship. It can be light without being frivolous, and dependable without being stiff.

## Anti-references

- Jira, Linear, Notion, Asana: cold, enterprise, impersonal. Too much chrome, zero warmth.
- Google Calendar, Outlook: sterile and transactional. Designed for offices, not homes.
- Tinder, Hinge, Bumble: matchmaking aesthetics — swipe mechanics, superficial motion, performance over depth. Together is about a relationship already in progress, not one being evaluated.
- Generic "couples apps" that feel like a startup template with a heart icon pasted on. Personalization must be real, not decorative.

## Design Principles

1. **Made for them, not adapted for them.** Every screen should feel like it was built for this specific pair, not a generic workspace with two seats. Personalization signals (names, avatars, shared context) should be visible and meaningful.
2. **Warmth without romance-app clichés.** Intimate and personal without the swipe-y, superficial energy of dating apps. The depth of a shared life, not the surface of a first impression.
3. **Playful but grounded.** Light touches of delight and personality, anchored by clarity and reliability. The app earns trust first; the playfulness is a bonus.
4. **Modern without cold.** Clean, contemporary UI that does not sacrifice warmth for minimalism. No sterile whites, no enterprise grey, no decoration-free voids.
5. **Clarity over cleverness.** The primary job is coordination. The UI gets out of the way when there is work to do, and adds personality in the moments between tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Ensure sufficient contrast on all text, including muted and placeholder text. Support reduced motion. Color is never the sole indicator of state.
